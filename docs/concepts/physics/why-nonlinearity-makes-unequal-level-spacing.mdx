# Why does nonlinearity make the energy level spacing inconsistent?

The reusable derivation from the Josephson cosine to unequal quantum level
spacing is owned by the SCQ_Design Knowledge Base:

- [Canonical knowledge: Josephson Cosine and Quantum Anharmonicity](https://github.com/arfiligol/SCQ_Design/blob/main/docs/knowledge/josephson-physics/josephson-cosine-and-quantum-anharmonicity.qmd)
- [Prerequisite: Josephson Current, Phase, Energy, and Inductance](https://github.com/arfiligol/SCQ_Design/blob/main/docs/knowledge/josephson-physics/josephson-current-phase-energy-and-inductance.qmd)

Use those nodes for the cosine expansion, the sign of the quartic correction,
the quantization assumptions, and the engineering meaning of anharmonicity.

This repository currently consumes Josephson nonlinearity in Julia Core circuit
authoring and harmonic-balance workflows. It does not currently provide a
quantum-model package or a quantum-model notebook that computes energy levels.

Do not duplicate reusable Josephson or quantum-anharmonicity theory here.
